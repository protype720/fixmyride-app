💡 Brake Lights Not Working

Step-by-step brake light troubleshooting guide.

Common Causes

  • Blown brake light fuse
  • Bad brake pedal switch
  • Burnt out bulbs
  • Bad rear ground
  • Trailer wiring damage
  • Broken wiring harness
  • Bad multifunction switch

Step-by-Step Checks

  1. Check if ANY brake lights work.
  2. Check brake light bulbs.
  3. Check brake light fuse.
  4. Check for power going INTO the brake switch.
  5. Press brake pedal and check power OUT of switch.
  6. Inspect rear grounds.
  7. Inspect trailer wiring for shorts or bad splices.
  8. Check rear wiring harness for damage.

Quick Tip

If tail lights work but brake lights do not, the problem is commonly the brake switch, brake fuse, or related wiring.
