🚗 6.0 Powerstroke Crank No Start Guide

Learn how to diagnose a Ford 6.0 Powerstroke that cranks but will not start.

1. Check Battery Voltage First

Weak batteries are one of the biggest causes of 6.0 Powerstroke starting problems.

  • Engine off should be around 12.6V
  • Cranking voltage should usually stay above 10V
  • Clean and tighten battery terminals
  • Check engine and frame grounds

2. Verify FICM Voltage

A healthy FICM should usually stay around 47–48V while cranking.

  • Low FICM voltage can stop injectors from firing
  • Weak batteries can lower FICM voltage
  • Check FICM connectors and wiring

3. Check ICP Pressure

The injectors need enough ICP pressure before the truck will start.

  • Check ICP sensor readings
  • Check IPR valve operation
  • Inspect for HPOP leaks
  • Check STC fitting issues

4. Check Cam/Crank Sync

If sync says NO while cranking, the truck may not start.

  • Check crank sensor
  • Check cam sensor
  • Inspect wiring harness damage
  • Check RPM signal while cranking

5. Fuel System Checks

  • Check fuel pump operation
  • Replace clogged fuel filters
  • Inspect fuel quality
  • Check fuel pressure
  • Inspect fuel pump fuse and relay

6. Common 6.0 No-Start Causes

  • Weak batteries
  • Low FICM voltage
  • Bad ICP sensor
  • Bad IPR valve
  • High pressure oil leak
  • Fuel delivery problems
  • Cam/crank sync issues
  • Damaged wiring harness
