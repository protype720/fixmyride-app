🔋 Battery Testing Guide

How to test batteries and charging systems.

Normal Voltage

  • 12.6V engine off
  • 13.5V–14.7V running
  • Below 10V cranking is bad

Common Problems

  • Slow crank
  • Clicking
  • Battery dies overnight
  • Needs jumped often

Testing Steps

  1. Check resting voltage
  2. Check voltage while cranking
  3. Check charging voltage running
  4. Clean terminals
  5. Inspect grounds and cables
