⚡ 6.0 FICM Voltage Guide

Normal FICM voltage and no-start testing.

Normal Voltage

  • 47–48V is healthy
  • Low voltage can stop injectors
  • Weak batteries can lower FICM voltage

Common Symptoms

  • Hard start
  • No start
  • Rough idle
  • Injector codes
  • White smoke

Checks

  1. Charge batteries fully
  2. Check cranking voltage
  3. Check FICM fuse
  4. Inspect FICM connectors
  5. Watch FICM voltage while cranking
