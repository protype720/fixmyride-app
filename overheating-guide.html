🔥 Car Overheating Guide

How to diagnose overheating problems step-by-step.

Common Causes

  • Low coolant
  • Coolant leak
  • Bad thermostat
  • Radiator fans not working
  • Weak water pump
  • Air trapped in cooling system
  • Clogged radiator
  • Head gasket issue

Step-by-Step Checks

  1. Let engine cool completely first.
  2. Check coolant level.
  3. Inspect for coolant leaks.
  4. Check radiator hoses.
  5. Verify radiator fans turn on.
  6. Check thermostat operation.
  7. Bleed air from cooling system.
  8. Inspect radiator flow.
  9. If oil and coolant are mixing, stop driving immediately.

Warning

Driving while overheating can destroy an engine. If the gauge is maxed out or steam is coming out, shut the vehicle off.
